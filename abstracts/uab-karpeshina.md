We consider Schrödinger operators H = −Δ + V(x) in ℝ^d, d ≥ 2, with quasi-periodic potentials V(x). We prove that the absolutely continuous spectrum of a generic H contains a semi-axis [λ*, ∞). We also construct a family of eigenfunctions of the absolutely continuous spectrum; these eigenfunctions are small perturbations of the exponentials. The existence of ballistic transport is proven. The proof is based on the multi-scale analysis in the momentum space. This is joint work with Leonid Parnovski and Roman Shterenberg.
