Call for talks

Faculty, postdocs, and undergraduate and graduate students from any University of Alabama System campus are invited to give a talk. Contributions on any topic in applied and computational mathematics are welcome.

What to send
Your name, affiliation, whether you are faculty or a student, the talk title, and a short abstract.
Where to send it
Email contact email to be announced.
Deadline
Saturday, October 24, 2026, for both talk submissions and registration.
Talk length
40 minutes for faculty talks and 20 minutes for student talks, including questions.
Attending without a talk
You are welcome. Please email the same address by October 24 so we can plan refreshments.
